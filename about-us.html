About Us

At Project Patent by Kaufhold and Dix Patent Law, we pride ourselves on delivering a comprehensive suite of intellectual property services tailored for inventors, creators, and businesses eager to protect and maximize the value of their innovations. Our seasoned team of patent attorneys combines extensive legal expertise with technical acumen, ensuring clients receive expert guidance through the often-complex landscape of patent and trademark processes. We understand that intellectual property is a vital asset, and we are committed to helping our clients navigate their journey from initial idea to market-ready protection.

What truly sets Project Patent apart is our commitment to transparency and reliability. We offer straightforward, flat-fee arrangements that cover all necessary legal services, eliminating the worry of hidden costs that can impact financial planning. Our clear approach to explaining the different types of protection available—utility, design, and plant patents—empowers clients to make informed decisions that best suit their innovations. With thousands of successful patents and trademarks under our belt, our track record speaks to our competence and dedication to client success.

Beyond just filing applications, we provide end-to-end support that includes initial evaluations of patentability, drafting, submission, and ongoing follow-up throughout the examination process. We prioritize clear communication, ensuring our clients are informed and supported at every step of the way. For innovators seeking not only legal protection but a strategic partner in nurturing their creative and technical assets, Project Patent stands ready to help turn ideas into valuable, protected opportunities in the marketplace.
